Scoring Framework — Fit Score (1–100)
Role Alignment 40%
How well the role maps to core PM skills and my experience level.
  • PM vs. APM vs. Senior PM fit
  • Domain match (B2B SaaS, PLG, fintech)
  • Scope: 0→1, growth, or platform
Company Stage 25%
Company maturity and how it shapes the PM opportunity.
  • Funding: Seed → Series B preferred
  • Product-led vs. sales-led motion
  • Engineering culture & PM autonomy
Growth Potential 20%
What I'll learn and how fast the role can accelerate my trajectory.
  • Breadth of ownership (strategy + execution)
  • Mentorship & PM team quality
  • Velocity — how fast does it ship?
Logistics 15%
Practical fit — location, comp band, and working model.
  • Remote / hybrid / on-site
  • Compensation range alignment
  • Toronto-based or time-zone compatible
